Add a widget

Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone Home Screen, open the widget gallery and search for TellyNook. Add a widget, then touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget to select a TV. The in-app widget guide also explains the steps.

A shortcut does not open its app

Check that the destination app is installed and that the saved link is correct. Some apps need a shortcut configured in Apple's Shortcuts app. If a direct link is unavailable, TellyNook may open the app's App Store page.

Organizing and locking

TellyNook creates shortcuts inside its own app. It does not automatically move or hide other apps on the iPhone Home Screen. Its passcode protects access within TellyNook; it does not lock other apps or restrict their use elsewhere.

Photos and privacy

Import photos with the system photo picker. TellyNook saves resized copies for its app and widgets. You can remove those copies without deleting your original photos. See our Privacy Policy for local storage, network requests and support email handling.
