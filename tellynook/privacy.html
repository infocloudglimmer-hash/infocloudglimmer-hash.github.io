TellyNook Privacy Policy

About this policy

TellyNook helps you organize app and website shortcuts and display photos in Home Screen widgets. This policy covers the iOS app and its widgets.

Developer: 陈以谢 (individual developer)

Privacy contact: infocloudglimmer@gmail.com

Policy version: 28 September 2026. Effective upon first publication.

Information stored on your device

TellyNook stores your TV names, notes, app and website shortcuts, album names, imported photos, display preferences and widget settings on your device. The app and its widgets share the library and imported photos through an iOS App Group container. TellyNook does not upload this library to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud synchronization service.

When you import photos, the app uses Apple's system photo picker and receives the images you select. It saves resized copies for display in the app and widgets. It does not request unrestricted access to your photo library or change your original photos.

Your TellyNook passcode is stored as a hash in the iOS Keychain, rather than as the original digits. If you use Face ID or your device passcode to reset it, iOS performs the authentication. TellyNook receives the result, not your biometric information or device passcode.

The app reads its own widget configurations to show setup guidance. It does not scan a list of every app installed on your device. Home Screen widgets may display the photos and names you select to anyone who can see your screen.

When the app connects to other services

Searching your saved library on the Home screen happens on your device. Searching the App Store while adding an app sends your search term and the country code derived from your device region to Apple's iTunes Search service.

When you add an App Store result, TellyNook may download the app's icon to determine a display color and request app-link configuration files from the developer's website and related subdomains to find an opening link.

When you enter a web link without a title, TellyNook may request that webpage to obtain its title, following redirects when necessary. For supported YouTube links, it may send the link to YouTube's oEmbed service to obtain the title. A URL can contain personal information, so avoid entering private or sensitive links if you do not want them sent to their destination service.

These requests go directly from your device to the relevant service. The recipient receives the requested URL or search parameters and ordinary network information such as your IP address and request headers. Its own privacy policy governs its handling of these requests, including server logs and retention. TellyNook does not control those logs.

Opening a shortcut passes the selected link to iOS and the destination app, browser or Shortcuts app. Your subsequent use of that service is governed by its privacy policy. Apple services are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); YouTube is covered by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Optional one-time purchase

TellyNook offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase to unlock all patterns and remove TV and custom-album creation limits. Apple processes payment. TellyNook uses StoreKit to read and verify purchase status on your device and in its widget extension, including restored purchases and refunds, so it can provide the features you purchased. It does not receive your payment card details or upload transaction records to a developer-operated server. You can restore purchases using the same Apple Account. Apple handles transaction data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and tracking

TellyNook does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, access the advertising identifier, or track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Saved library information remains on your device until you change or delete it or remove the app's local data. You can delete saved app entries, imported photos and user-created albums in the app. Removing a shortcut from one TV or deleting that TV does not necessarily remove the saved app entry from the overall library. Deleting imported photos in TellyNook does not delete their originals in Apple Photos.

If the library file becomes unreadable, the app may preserve a local recovery copy. Device backups and restored copies are managed by iOS and your backup settings; TellyNook does not operate those backup services. Keychain items can persist after uninstalling the app, so uninstalling is not a promise that every stored item has been erased. You can replace the TellyNook passcode through its reset flow after device authentication.

You can use saved content without performing App Store searches or importing web links. Canceling the system photo picker avoids importing photos; deleting an imported copy removes it from TellyNook. Remove widgets if you no longer want their contents shown on the Home Screen.

For requests about data held by a destination service, contact that service. For questions about TellyNook or applicable privacy rights, contact infocloudglimmer@gmail.com. We cannot remotely access or delete the library stored on your device.
